## Reproducible procedure

witnesses is literally replayed through the exact engine. The resulting 18
canonical boundary hashes are all distinct and agree with the historical
corpus. Every state is then processed in this order:

1. Round-30 coarse capacity (`need > 5(O_cap+R_cap)+4`);
2. if it survives, exact macro DFS with Area A's exact prune rules and the
   independently recomputed Round-32 B+R bound;
3. no phase-walk capacity helper, table, or phase-derived pruning.
